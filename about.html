The Tenderfy Method

A better way to tender.

Winning work isn't just about answering questions — it's a clear, consistent process every member of your team can follow. The Tenderfy Method is our industry-recommended, AI-enabled tendering SOP, and how to run each stage inside Tenderfy.

  • Go / No-Go — commit to the bids worth winning
  • Task list & delivery plan — before a word is written
