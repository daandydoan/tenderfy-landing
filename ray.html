Ray understands a real tender, holds the context, and takes real actions inside Tenderfy — using the same tools and content your team already relies on.

Reads the tender pack

Ray reviews every document and works out what the tender is actually asking for.

Finds requirements & criteria

Requirements, evaluation criteria and mandatory schedules — surfaced and structured, not buried.

Searches your content

Ray searches your Library, past projects and resumes for the content that fits this tender.
